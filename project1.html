About The Tyndall Effect

Understanding the science behind beautiful light beams.

The Tyndall Effect is the scattering of light by particles in a colloid or fine suspension. When a beam of light passes through these particles, some of the light is scattered in different directions, making its path visible.


From mysterious forest light rays to glowing beams in a dusty room, this phenomenon reveals how science can create some of nature's most beautiful visual experiences.

The Science Of Light

Four simple steps behind the phenomenon.

💡

Light Source

A beam of light enters a medium containing tiny suspended particles.

🔬

Particles

Tiny particles interact with the light as it travels through the medium.

✨

Scattering

Light is scattered in different directions by the suspended particles.

🌌

Visible Light

The scattered light makes the path of the beam visible to an observer.

Light In Everyday Life

Discover the phenomenon around you.

🌲 Forest Light Rays

Sunlight scattering through mist and airborne particles creates beautiful visible rays between trees.

🌫️ Foggy Atmosphere

Light from headlights and lamps becomes visible as it scatters through tiny suspended water droplets.

🔦 Dusty Rooms

Sunlight entering a dark room can reveal a bright beam when dust particles scatter the light.

🧪 Colloidal Solutions

A flashlight passing through a suitable colloidal solution can reveal the path of light.
